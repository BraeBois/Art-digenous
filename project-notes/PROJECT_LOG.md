   What surprised me most was how easy it was once i understood what i was doing.  I gave the AI my idea and it ran with it, of course it didn't have the same reasoning capacity as a human and so I had to guide it constantly but once i figured out how to prompt it properly it was as simple as point and shoot for the most part. Another thing that surprised me was how interconnected the copilot AI was with all the other software and how quickly it would update documents even on other devices. At one point i published my link, copied it onto my phone and sen it to a friend to see if they could open it, they could but then i made a change and committed it and within a minute the change had made it to the third device.
3. Which parts were easy, and which were hard? Why do you think that is?
   The hardest parts about this project were coming up with an idea and learning how to use the software. Creating not only an original idea on my own but making sure it fit the criteria and would tie in well to the concepts we learned in class was harder than I imagined. I think thats because I had to do it myself and did not use the help of artificial intelligence, and learning the software was difficult partially because of its complexity and it is an entirely new world to me, but also because i was using the incorrect software for the first few weeks of my project.
    Working with  the AI was relatively easy on copilot but the AI on the Github website where I originally wrote my code was a huge pain. I think this is because the actual app is meant to be coded on and was created for that exact reason. As well as it had acces to the instructions you provided so it knew already what to do and what not to do.
5. If a competitor had the same AI tool, how long would it take them to copy your site?
   Assuming we are looking at a fast follower type of problem, like any fast follower it would not take them long to copy or create a similar site. However I hope that by being the first in the market I would be able to create my cross sided network effects and solidify my value chain. Copying a website or app is easy as this project has shown, but my platform offers 2 sided benefits and that raises the switching cost.
   And I have already seeded the market with complements and encouraged the development of complementary goods as we learned in chapter 10, by providing a platform for the buying and selling of jewelry, and if I have pre established distrubution channels with supplies before rivals can copy my site it will make it harder for them to do so.
