The idea behind the idea

A better path from maker to admirer.

Art-Digenous is a student concept for a two-sided marketplace: a discovery space shaped around artists and the people looking for their work.

01

The challenge

Independent makers can be hard to discover beyond their existing circles. Buyers may not know where to find artists or learn about work directly from its maker.

02

The concept

A focused directory and marketplace could bring discovery, maker profiles, and work together—while giving artists control over their stories.

03

A possible model

A real business might earn a transparent fee on completed sales, with clear artist consent and fair terms. No sales or fees happen in this prototype.

The network effect

More makers. More reasons to visit.

Artists bring original work and audiences; more choice can make the platform more useful to buyers. Reaching the first buyers and makers is the hard part.

Before anything goes live

Built with care, not assumptions.

  • Indigenous-led consultation and governance
  • Artist consent, fair compensation, and transparent fees
  • Strong privacy, secure payments, and dispute support
  • Moderation against misrepresentation and unauthorized listings

Help and information

Support.

Find answers about refunds, returns, and this marketplace concept. This student prototype has no checkout, orders, or customer-support service.

Prototype notice

All listings and people are fictional. No purchases, refunds, returns, or support requests can be processed here. These pages describe considerations for a possible future marketplace, not active policies.

Refunds

There are no charges to refund.

This prototype does not take payments or create orders, so it cannot issue refunds. A real marketplace would need a clear refund policy before launch, including timelines, who handles each case, and how buyers and artists can resolve disputes.

Any future policy should be developed transparently with participating artists and buyers rather than assumed in advance.

Returns

Returns are not available in this demo.

Because no real items are sold here, there are no orders to return. A future marketplace would need to explain return eligibility, condition requirements, shipping costs, timelines, and how unique or made-to-order pieces are handled.

Those terms should be agreed with artists and shown to buyers before any real purchase.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions.

Can I buy artwork or jewelry here?

No. This is a student prototype with fictional listings. It has no checkout, accounts, or payment processing.

Can I request a refund or return?

No orders can be placed through this demo, so refunds and returns cannot be processed. A real marketplace would need clear, artist-informed policies.

Are the artists and listings real?

No. All people, profiles, and listings shown on this site are fictional examples.

How can I contact an artist or get help with an order?

This prototype does not connect to artists or provide order support. A real service would need consent-based contact options and a staffed process for questions and disputes.
